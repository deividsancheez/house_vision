## How the lookbook and the 3D plan work together

- `products.json` here is the lookbook catalog. The 3D plan's `products.json` is not touched.
- Products already in the 3D plan keep their exact 3D ids (for example `LR-RUG-A`, `BR-DESK-A`) and use the same 3D-plan photos and sizes. New products use `LB-<CATEGORY>-NN` ids, which never collide.
- Each product has `slot_3d` and `placement_3d`. `slot` means it can swap into that slot in the 3D rooms (`LR-ROD|BR-ROD` means either room). `free placement` means it lands in the 3D inventory and you place it anywhere (trays, vases, baskets, side tables, benches, lumbar pillows, stone accents, the second bedside lamp).
- Sizes are in inches (`w_in`, `d_in`, `h_in`). Values marked in `estimated` were not printed on the product page (for example rug and curtain thickness). If a size is `null`, the 3D app must ask for it instead of guessing; those items are on the MANUAL ACTION NEEDED list.
- `main_color_hex` is sampled from the product photo (approximate). `model_3d_url` is IKEA's own 3D model when IKEA offers one.
